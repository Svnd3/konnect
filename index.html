Questions
You asked, we answered.
What is the KSh 100 reliability check? +
It's a one-time M-PESA payment that verifies you're a real, committed member. It's not a subscription fee — you pay it once, forever. Hosts can see your verified badge before accepting you.
How does escrow protect me? +
When you pay monthly, your money sits with Konnect — not the host. You then confirm access in-app. Only after confirmation does the host receive payment. If access isn't given, you get a refund.
What subscriptions can I share on Konnect? +
Spotify, ChatGPT, Canva Pro, YouTube Premium, Netflix, Prime Video, Duolingo Max, Proton VPN, and more. Hosts can list any service that supports family or team plans.
What if I have a dispute with my host? +
Report it in-app before confirming access. Our team reviews the case and can freeze the escrow, intervene, or issue a refund — unlike other platforms where you're on your own.
Can I host my own family on Konnect? +
Yes! Create a listing, set your price per slot, and Konnect will match you with verified members. You'll receive monthly M-PESA payments automatically once members confirm access.
